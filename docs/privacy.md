### 3. Speicherung in deinem Browser
Die Web-App setzt **keine Cookies**.
- Wenn du in den Einstellungen Spruch oder Sprache wählst, speichert dein Browser das lokal („localStorage“).

Beides verlässt deinen Browser nicht und ist für die von dir gewünschten Funktionen unbedingt erforderlich (§ 25 Abs. 2 Nr. 2 TDDDG). Du kannst es jederzeit über die Website-Daten deines Browsers löschen.

### 4. Standort

- **Apps:** Nur wenn du die Standortberechtigung erteilst („bei Nutzung der App“, ungefährer Standort).
- **Web-App:** Erst wenn du auf „Wie weit?“ tippst und es im Browser erlaubst. Hast du es einmal erlaubt, nutzt die Seite die Erlaubnis bei späteren Besuchen wieder, bis du sie in den Browser-Einstellungen widerrufst.
- Der Standort wird **nur auf deinem Gerät** für diese Berechnung verwendet, **nicht gespeichert und nicht übertragen** – weder an uns noch an Dritte.
- Rechtsgrundlage ist deine Einwilligung (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. a DSGVO), die du jederzeit in den System- bzw. Browser-Einstellungen widerrufen kannst. Ohne Erlaubnis funktioniert alles genauso, nur ohne Entfernungsangabe.

### 2. Loading the web app (hosting)
The web app is served by **GitHub Pages** of GitHub, Inc. (USA). When you open it, GitHub processes technically necessary data – your IP address, time, requested file and browser details – to deliver the page and keep the service secure. We have no access to this data.
- Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is serving the page securely and reliably.
- GitHub is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, so the European Commission's adequacy decision (Art. 45 GDPR) covers the transfer to the USA.
- Details: [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

Font, flags and data are served from the same site; no third-party services are embedded.

### 3. Storage in your browser
The web app sets **no cookies**.
- When you choose a saying or language in the settings, your browser stores it locally ("localStorage").
- For offline use, the browser keeps a copy of the page in its cache.

Neither leaves your browser, and both are strictly necessary for features you asked for (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). You can delete them at any time via your browser's site data.

### 4. Location
The apps and the web app only use your location to show the **distance** to the place where it is beer o'clock right now.
- **Apps:** Only if you grant the location permission ("while using the app", approximate location).
- **Web app:** Only once you tap "How far?" and allow it in the browser. Once allowed, the page uses the permission again on later visits until you revoke it in your browser settings.
- The location is used **only on your device** for this calculation and is **neither stored nor transmitted** – not to us, not to anyone else.
- The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time in the system or browser settings. Without it everything works the same, just without the distance.

### 5. Settings in the apps
Your choice of saying (4 or 5 p.m.) is stored locally on the device and never leaves it.

### 6. Contact by email
If you write to us, we only use your email address and message to reply (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and delete them once the matter is settled.

### 7. Testing on Google Play
Testers of the Android app join a Google Group. As its manager we can see the members' email addresses. We only use them to run the test (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and don't pass them on. You can leave the group at any time; we delete the group when the test ends. [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) applies to the group itself.

### 8. Distribution via app stores
Downloads via Google Play or the App Store are subject to Google's or Apple's privacy policies. We only receive anonymous, aggregated statistics from them (e.g. number of installs).

### 9. Links to other services
The apps and the web app link to Google Maps or Apple Maps, to Untappd and to brewery websites. Only when you tap a link does the respective app or website open; their privacy policies apply there. Only the search (brewery or place name) is passed on, not your location.

You can object to processing based on Art. 6(1)(f) (Art. 21) and withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)). Just write to the address above.
